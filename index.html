# shannonme 📡 *Information as surprise — the measure of what could not be predicted, received as the price and the gift of news.* shannonme is two true roots welded into one word: **shannon**, from Claude Shannon's 1948 founding of information theory — the proof that the information in a message is its *surprise* (self-information -log p; source-entropy H = -Σ p·log p, in bits) — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So shannonme is surprise carried as a decree: information severed from meaning and bound instead to the unpredicted. The -me grade is exact — surprise is the one measure the receiver structurally cannot author, because anything you could have produced from your own model carries, by definition, zero surprise. News is received or it is not news. shannonme names the **source-side measure** in the cathedral's mathema/info quadrille: it weighs the source's expected surprise where **kolmogorovme** weighs a single object, **landauerme** prices erasure, and **freenergyme** names the drive to minimize surprise. Under shannonme, a fully expected message carries nothing; news is exactly what could not be predicted; redundancy is not waste but care — saying it twice so the storm cannot eat it; and error-correction is grace built into a message before the noise arrives. Its light is the honor it pays the unforeseen — why love that surprises carries most. Its shadow is novelty-addiction (noise mistaken for news, since pure randomness is *maximally* surprising) and the possessor's flattening that compresses the beloved into a predictable model and calls that knowing them.
